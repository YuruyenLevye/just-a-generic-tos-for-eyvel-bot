# Eyvel Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Eyvel Bot** ("Eyvel", "Bot", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use or interact with the Bot.

Eyvel is designed with privacy in mind and follows a minimal-data approach. We only store information that is necessary for the Bot's functionality.

## 1. Information We Store

Eyvel stores **Discord User IDs** when necessary to provide certain features.

The Discord User ID is used to associate stored game, entertainment, or feature-related data with the correct Discord user.

Other than Discord User IDs and data directly required for the operation of specific features, Eyvel does not intentionally store personal information about users.

## 2. Information We Do Not Store

Eyvel does not intentionally store the following information in its database:

* Discord usernames
* Display names
* Profile pictures or avatars
* Email addresses
* IP addresses
* Passwords
* Discord authentication tokens
* Private messages
* Message content
* Voice data or recordings
* Physical location data
* Payment information
* Contact information

## 3. Discord API Data

Eyvel may request certain information from Discord through the official Discord API when required for a feature.

For example, if an Eyvel feature displays a user's Discord profile picture, the profile picture may be retrieved directly from Discord's API.

**Eyvel does not store retrieved profile pictures or other temporary Discord profile information in its own database unless explicitly stated otherwise.**

Such information may only be processed temporarily as necessary for the requested feature.

The availability and handling of information provided by Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies.

## 4. How We Use Discord User IDs

Stored Discord User IDs may be used to:

* Identify users within Eyvel's features
* Store and retrieve entertainment-related data
* Maintain user-specific statistics or progress
* Apply feature-specific cooldowns or limits
* Prevent abuse of the Bot
* Maintain the functionality and reliability of Eyvel

Discord User IDs are not sold, rented, or used for advertising purposes.

## 5. Entertainment and Game Data

Eyvel may store data associated with entertainment or game features.

Such data is associated with a Discord User ID and is used solely to provide the relevant feature.

Examples may include game progress, scores, statistics, cooldowns, balances, or other feature-specific values.

No additional personal information is intentionally required to maintain this data.

## 6. Message Content

Eyvel may temporarily process information received through Discord when responding to commands or interactions.

However, **message content is not intentionally stored in Eyvel's database**.

Information processed temporarily to execute a command is discarded when it is no longer required for that operation.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information.

We do not intentionally share stored Discord User IDs with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

Information may only be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

* Operate and maintain Eyvel
* Protect the security of the Bot
* Prevent abuse or unauthorized access
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

## 8. Data Retention

Discord User IDs and associated feature data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant Eyvel features.

If the information is no longer required, it may be deleted.

Data may also be deleted when a feature is discontinued or when it is otherwise no longer necessary for the operation of Eyvel.

## 9. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with their Discord User ID.

To request deletion, contact us using the information provided below and include your Discord User ID.

After receiving and verifying the request, we will take reasonable steps to delete the relevant stored data, unless retention is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.

## 10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Third-Party Services

Eyvel operates through Discord and may use Discord's API to provide its features.

Discord independently processes information according to its own policies and terms.

Eyvel is not responsible for information collected or processed directly by Discord or other third-party services.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Eyvel does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and all applicable laws when using Discord and Eyvel.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the **Last Updated** date will be updated accordingly.

Continued use of Eyvel after changes to this Privacy Policy become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
